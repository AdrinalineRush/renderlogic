Founder & Creative Director

Achu Vijayan works where cinema, color, and software meet.

Achu is a filmmaker, editor, certified DaVinci Resolve colorist, and software engineer based in Kerala. He founded RenderLogic to turn production experience into both finished screen work and practical tools for creators.

One practice, two connected disciplines

Cinema

Story, pacing, and visual finish

Over more than a decade in the Indian film industry, Achu has worked across editing, directing, co-production, and visual finishing. Credits represented here include Vichithram, Charles Enterprises, Aattam, and Mukundan Unni Associates.

Software

Tools grounded in production reality

RenderLogic's product work focuses on local AI, speech recognition, workflow utilities, and creative media systems. The goal is to make technically powerful software feel direct, fast, and useful in everyday work.
